The View

Valle Justina sits at the top of Mayong Payong, high enough that the whole of Masbate opens up beneath you. The infinity pool runs straight out into the horizon.

Cool air. Wide sky. Nothing in a hurry.

Noon

Golden Hour

The ridge turns warm and the pool goes to gold.

After Dark

Questions

Before you come up.

How do I reserve, and how do I pay?

Pick your dates on the calendar, send the request, and the resort will confirm. Sending the form is a request, not a confirmed booking, so please wait for the reply before treating it as final. Reservations are secured by full payment in advance, by BDO transfer or GCash. Send your proof of payment with your reference number.

How do I get the 10% discount?

Book at least one month before your check-in date. The calculator above applies it automatically once your check-in is 30 or more days away.

What is included in the room rate?

Air-conditioning, bunk beds for 4 to 5 guests in a Standard Room or 2 to 3 in the Balcony Room, free pool use for 4 guests, and complimentary breakfast for 2.

Can I bring extra guests?

Yes. Extra guest and extra pool guest charges are to be confirmed. Tell us your headcount in the notes and the resort will quote you.

Can I come up just for the day?

Yes. Entrance is ₱50 per person. Swimming is ₱250 in the daytime and ₱300 at night, and it is free for guests staying overnight. No reservation needed, and you can settle the day pass by GCash before you drive up. Opening hours are to be confirmed.

What do I need for camping?

Camping is sold per pitch and you bring your own tent. The per-pitch rate is to be confirmed.

Can I book the whole place for an event?

Yes. Exclusive use of the entire property is ₱50,000. Message the resort with your date and what you have in mind.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check in from 2:00 PM. Check out by 11:00 AM.
