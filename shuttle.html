Round trip shuttle

Door to door between Masbate City center and the resort. One flat fare for the whole vehicle, not per person.

Fare

Capacity
Up to 8 people per trip

The fare is flat for the trip, whether it is 1 person or 8. Pick a group up to your size and split it among yourselves. Send the payment first, then enter the reference below and this page issues your ticket right away. We will confirm the exact pickup time with you by Messenger or call after you send your request — the time below is your preference, not a fixed schedule.
